PRODUCT INFORMATION

Standard Plan

The Standard Plan provides access to basic customer support and standard service features.

Premium Plan

The Premium Plan includes priority customer support and access to premium service features.

Premium customers receive priority assistance when contacting customer support.

Delivery

Standard orders are normally processed using standard delivery.

Premium orders are eligible for priority processing and may be delivered within two business days when priority delivery is available.

Product Support

Customers experiencing a problem with a product should provide the product name, order number, and a description of the issue.

Warranty

Warranty eligibility depends on the product and purchase conditions. Customers should provide their order details when requesting warranty support.

Express Replacement

Premium customers with eligible defective products can request an express replacement. Approved express replacements are normally processed within one business day.
