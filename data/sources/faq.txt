FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How can I request a refund?

A refund request can be submitted to customer support within 30 days of purchase. Customers should provide their order number.

Can I cancel my order?

An order can normally be cancelled before it has been shipped.

How do I contact customer support?

Customers can contact the customer support team for assistance with orders, refunds, returns, accounts, and product issues.

What information should I provide for a product problem?

Provide the product name, order number, and a description of the problem.

Do premium customers receive priority support?

Yes. Premium customers receive priority customer support.

How quickly are premium orders delivered?

Premium orders may qualify for priority delivery within two business days when the service is available.

What should I do if I notice suspicious activity on my account?

Change your password and contact customer support. Never share passwords or verification codes with another person.

Can customers change their delivery address?

Customers may request a delivery address change before the order has been shipped. Once shipping has started, changing the delivery address may not be possible.
