CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICIES

Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. A valid order number is required to process the refund. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel an order before it has been shipped. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be available and the customer should follow the return procedure.

Return Policy

Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Products should be returned in their original condition. Certain personalized or final-sale products may not be eligible for return.

Customer Support

Customer support is available Monday through Saturday. Customers can contact support for order questions, refunds, returns, account issues, and product-related assistance.

Complaint Handling

Customer complaints should include the order number and a clear description of the problem. Complaints are reviewed by the customer support team and may be escalated when additional investigation is required.

Account Security

Customers should never share passwords or verification codes with another person. If suspicious activity is detected, the customer should change their password and contact customer support.
